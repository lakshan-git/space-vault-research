About SpaceVault Storage

SpaceVault Storage is a Dubai-based self-storage and logistics company established in 2023, operating under Space Vault Storage Cargo Packaging L.L.C — a UAE Mainland entity registered with the Dubai DED. Located in the strategically central Al Quoz Industrial Area 4, the company offers a full spectrum of storage solutions: from compact personal lockers to large business inventory units, paired with professional cargo packaging and comprehensive moving & relocation support.

With climate-controlled facilities, 24/7 CCTV surveillance, flexible rental terms, and a customer-first approach, SpaceVault has quickly built a reputation as a reliable, competitively priced alternative to larger storage chains. The company is actively expanding its digital presence across Google, social media, and third-party directories, with overwhelmingly positive early reviews from satisfied customers.
